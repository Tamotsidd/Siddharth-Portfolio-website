About

The person behind the camera — how I got here and what I'm chasing.

about-portrait.jpg

I started with a borrowed camera and a city I wanted to understand better. Since then my work has moved between quiet documentary portraits and the controlled chaos of weddings — two things that, it turns out, ask for the same patience.

I shoot mostly on location, with natural light where I can get it. My favourite frame is always the one nobody was posing for.

When I'm not working, I'm usually walking the same three streets in Damak looking for light I haven't seen before.
